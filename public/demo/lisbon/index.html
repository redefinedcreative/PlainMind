Three slow days in Lisbon

8 min read

Lisbon rewards the unhurried. Start where the city starts its own day: a pastel de nata standing at a counter, the 28 tram grinding up toward Graça, and the river turning from grey to silver below.

Day one belongs to Alfama. Get lost on purpose. The streets fold back on themselves, and every wrong turn ends at a miradouro with a better view than the last.

Day two, cross to Belém by mid-morning, before the crowds, and come back for a long lunch in a room with six tables and no menu in English.

Day three, do less. Sit by the river at Cais do Sodré, take the ferry across for the view back, and let the city come to you.
